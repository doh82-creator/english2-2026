39번 문장 삽입

정부 발행 화폐의 문제와 공급이 제한된 귀금속 화폐라는 해결책 사이의 연결을 찾으세요.

39 글의 흐름으로 보아, 주어진 문장이 들어가기에 가장 적절한 곳을 고르시오.
주어진 문장

For this reason, many countries have preferred using gold, silver, or some other material that is inherently limited in supply, as money.

The big problem with money created by the government is that those who run the government always face the temptation to create more money and spend it. ① Whether among ancient kings or modern politicians, this has happened again and again over the centuries, leading to inflation and the many economic and social problems that follow from inflation. ② It is a way of depriving governments of the power to expand the money supply to inflationary levels. ③ Gold has long been considered ideal for this purpose, since the supply of gold in the world usually cannot be increased rapidly. ④ When paper money is convertible into gold whenever the individual chooses to do so, then the money is said to be “backed up” by gold. ⑤ This expression is misleading only if we imagine that the value of the gold is somehow transferred to the paper money, when in fact the real point is that the gold simply limits the amount of paper money that can be issued.

3. 핵심 구문·문법

분사구문 ..., leading to inflation

앞 절 전체의 결과를 나타내며 “그 결과 인플레이션을 초래하면서”로 해석한다.

prefer -ing have preferred using gold

prefer의 목적어로 동명사 using이 쓰였다.

deprive A of B depriving governments of the power

정부에게서 화폐 공급 확대 권한을 빼앗는다는 구조다.

when의 대조 용법 ..., when in fact the real point is ...

시간보다 “그런데 사실”이라는 대조를 나타낸다.
