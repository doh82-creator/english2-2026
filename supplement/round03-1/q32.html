32다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?

Many early dot-com investors focused almost entirely on revenue growth instead of net income. Many early dot-com companies earned most of their revenue from selling advertising space on their Web sites. To boost reported revenue, some sites began exchanging ad space. Company A would put an ad for its Web site on company B’s Web site, and company B would put an ad for its Web site on company A’s Web site. No money ever changed hands, but each company recorded revenue (for the value of the space that it gave up on its site) and expense (for the value of its ad that it placed on the other company’s site). This practice did little to boost net income and —but it did boost reported revenue. This practice was quickly put to an end because accountants felt that it did not meet the criteria of the revenue recognition principle.

1. 선택지별 판단

Many early dot-com investors focused almost entirely on revenue growth instead of net income. Many early dot-com companies earned most of their revenue from selling advertising space on their Web sites. To boost reported revenue, some sites began exchanging ad space. Company A would put an ad for its Web site on company B’s Web site, and company B would put an ad for its Web site on company A’s Web site. No money ever changed hands, but each company recorded revenue (for the value of the space that it gave up on its site) and expense (for the value of its ad that it placed on the other company’s site). This practice did little to boost net income and —but it did boost reported revenue. This practice was quickly put to an end because accountants felt that it did not meet the criteria of the revenue recognition principle.

* revenue: 수익　** net income: 순이익

선택지표현판단과 근거
①simplified the Web design process
웹 디자인 과정을 단순화했다		오답. 광고 교환과 웹 디자인 절차의 관계는 제시되지 않는다.
②resulted in no additional cash inflow
추가 현금 유입을 만들지 못했다		정답. No money ever changed hands를 정확히 바꾸어 쓴 표현이다.
③decreased the salaries of the employees
직원 급여를 감소시켰다		오답. 비용 기록은 광고 가치에 관한 것이며 직원 급여가 아니다.
④intensified competition among companies
기업 간 경쟁을 심화했다		오답. 경쟁은 언급되지 않고, A와 B가 서로 광고 공간을 교환한다.
⑤triggered conflicts on the content of Web ads
웹 광고 내용에 관한 갈등을 촉발했다		오답. 중단 이유는 갈등이 아니라 수익 인식 원칙에 맞지 않았기 때문이다.
