결과가 같은데도 시간과 노력에 더 큰 가치를 부여하는 역설을 읽으세요.

32다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오. [3점]

It’s hard to pay more for the speedy but highly skilled person, simply because there’s less effort being observed. Two researchers once did a study in which they asked people how much they would pay for data recovery. They found that people would pay a little more for a greater quantity of rescued data, but what they were most sensitive to was the number of hours the technician worked. When the data recovery took only a few minutes, willingness to pay was low, but when it took more than a week to recover the same amount of data, people were willing to pay much more. Think about it: They were willing to pay more for the slower service with the same outcome. Fundamentally, when we __________, we’re paying for incompetence. Although it is actually irrational, we feel more rational, and more comfortable, paying for incompetence.

1. 문맥의 핵심 흐름

도입: 주제 제시

빠르고 숙련된 사람에게 더 지불하기 어려움

관찰되는 노력이 적기 때문

▶
예시: 데이터 복구 실험

복구량보다 작업 시간에 민감

몇 분 → 낮은 지불 / 일주일 이상 → 훨씬 많이 지불

▶
시사점

실험 결과

같은 결과의 더 느린 서비스에 더 지불

▶
결론

결과보다 노력 중시 (빈칸) = 무능에 지불

비합리적이지만 더 편안하게 느낌

한 문장 핵심사람들은 서비스의 결과보다 투입된 시간과 노력을 더 중시하여, 동일한 결과를 느리게 낸 비숙련에 더 많은 돈을 낸다.
